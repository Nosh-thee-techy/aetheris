02

Why this place was counted as flooded

Seven people were evacuated and more than 40 were marooned in 20 households in Kadibo sub-county. No village is named. This is not a claim that every part of Kadibo flooded.

The Star, reported 4–5 May 2024. A news report is a label, not a survey.

    04

    What can be done with this claim

    Five things can happen to a claim, and each belongs to someone. The buttons enable themselves only when the contract would accept them, judged by the chain’s clock. A refusal is explained in words.

    To try it you need a browser wallet on Sepolia with a little test ETH: 0.01 to challenge, plus gas. Reading needs no wallet. If you challenge, the referee key is one key held by the project team, and it has 48 hours to decide. If it stays silent the claim stands and your stake comes back.

    The identifiers, so you can recompute them

    The claim id is keccak256(zoneId, eventWeek, ruleId, thresholdsHash). Change the rule and the id changes, so a claim cannot be moved onto a different rule.
