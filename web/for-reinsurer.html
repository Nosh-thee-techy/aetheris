Reinsurer workspace · event loss

Nyando basin, 4–6 May 2024. One event. Not a catastrophe model.

A cat model needs thousands of simulated years, a vulnerability function, and your own exposure file. What we can give you honestly is the footprint of one named flood, layered the way you already read an event: hazard, exposure, modeled loss, and the residual the rule left unmodeled. Put it in your model. Do not treat this page as the model.

05

What a catastrophe model still has to do

This page is the event you would feed the model. It is not the model.

  • No stochastic catalogue.One week. No average annual loss, no occurrence or aggregate exceedance curve, no probable maximum loss, no return period.
  • No vulnerability function.We do not convert open-water fraction into damage. The payout is a binary grant.
  • No real exposure file.0.001 ETH per place is a demo so the escrow can move. Load your own sums insured.
  • News is not ground-up loss.The seven names are places reporters reached. Unknown is not “no flood”.
