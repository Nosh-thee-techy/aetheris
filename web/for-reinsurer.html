Reinsurer workspace · event loss

Nyando basin, 4–6 May 2024. One event. Not a catastrophe model.

A cat model needs thousands of simulated years, a vulnerability function, and your own exposure file. What we can give you honestly is the footprint of one named flood, layered the way you already read an event: hazard, exposure, modeled loss, and the residual the rule left unmodeled. Put it in your model. Do not treat this page as the model.

Vulnerability · the damage function

Depth × a JRC curve for a housing class → damage ratio → ratio × TIV. Radar open-water fraction is not depth and is not used here.

Depth proxy

0.9 m

adapted at Ahero: “waist-high in houses used by officers at Ahero Police Station”, Nyando Deputy County Commissioner Elijah Maranga, Kenya News Agency, 5 May 2024. Waist-high is taken as 0.9 m. assumption at the other six places: the same river, the same week, no local depth, so Ahero’s number is borrowed. This is not a measured flood-depth map.

06

What a catastrophe model still has to do

This page is the event you would feed the model. It is not the model.

  • No measured flood-depth map.One reported “waist-high” at Ahero, converted to 0.9 m. Radar fraction is still not depth.
  • No stochastic catalogue.One week. No average annual loss, no occurrence or aggregate exceedance curve, no probable maximum loss, no return period.
  • TIV is a stand-in.Households × a Kakuma shelter grant. Load your own sums insured. Four places have no TIV at all.
  • News is not a loss survey.The seven names are places reporters reached. Unknown is not “no flood”.
