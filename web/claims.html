Claims · Nyando basin · 4–6 May 2024

Seven places, one flood week

News reports named these seven places as flooded. For each one, the rule asks radar and rainfall whether they sign. Where both do, a claim opens on the testnet and anyone can challenge it. Pick a place to see the evidence and the actions open to you.
